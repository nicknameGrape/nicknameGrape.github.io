The Greedy Monkey King
The greedy monkey king is eaten by a lion.
Notes for the StorytellerThis story has no text, so use your own words to tell the story. Basically, the greedy monkey king won't let the other monkeys eat bananas. Each day, they try to take one and he yells at them. A lion notices this and hides behind the banana bush on the third day.
Monkeys and Lion Browser Game
Read the story "The Greedy Monkey King", then play this browser game! Invite brave students to take the role of monkeys and grab their favorite fruits. There is a lion hiding behind one of the bushes.
Controls If your device has a touch screen, touch a bush to take the fruit. If you do not have a touch screen, use the mouse or use the number keys: 1 is the leftmost fruit, then 2, and so on.

When is your birthday?

12 Months
Birthday Bingo
40 unique bingo cards with all 12 months and some dates.
How ToPrint out A3-size as the numbers are quite small. Cut. Distribute. Pick a student at random and ask "When is your birthday?" Circle both the month and the date on the bingo card. If you are lucky, your card will have both.
Birth-weekday
Do you know which day of the week you were born on?
How to PlayFirst, you will be prompted to enter the school year (学年) in which you were born. The Japanese school year starts in April and ends in March, so if you were born in January, February, or March, enter the year before you were born. This way, when you are doing this with a class, you can ask every kid their birthday and not have to worry about changing the year. Just click on a month, then click on a day, and the day of the week will be returned.

Good Boy/Bad Boy
A Rock-Paper-Scissors Game played in groups of three.
How to PlayMake groups of three. One player is the boy, one is the angel, and one is the devil. "The boy" asks, "What time is it?". "The angel" replies, "It's study time." "The devil" counters, "No, no, no. It's talking time." The angel and the devil play rock-paper-scissors. If the angel wins, the boy does the right thing (circle "⭕"). If the devil wins, the boy does the wrong thing (circle "❌"). If they tie, the boy decides what to do.
