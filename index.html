Hey, you! Let's play.
A lonely kid is invited to play dodgeball.
Hey, you! Let's play...
Invite a "lonely kid" to join a game.
How to Play Pick a student to be the "lonely kid". The other students take turns inviting him or her to join a game. The "lonely kid" taps the picture to reveal the reply. Most of the games result in "No, thank you.", but three of the choices will result in "OK!". The student who succeeds becomes the next "lonely kid".

Alphabet

Peel and See
Peel the alphabet letters one by one until you can guess what is hidden behind.
Keyboard Controls There are various options toggled with keyboard keys. For example, switch between upper and lower case with the C key. Press Spacebar to start. Press a letter key to remove that letter. Press Spacebar again to reveal the entire picture. Press Enter to start over.
Mirrored Letters, Side-to-Side
Draw alphabet letters that are symmetrical side-to-side.
Mirrored Letters, Top-to-Bottom
Draw alphabet letters that are symmetrical top-to-bottom.
Touch the Sign (Let's Try 1)
Find a word hidden in a picture of a town. The picture is from the Grade 3 English Textbook Let's Try 1.
How to Play Tap the orange area on the right to show a prompt. Then touch the matching word in the drawing. Faster matches get more points, but you only get three tries. *Note: You must touch the word, not the picture.

Reading

Reading Practice Sheets
Download a .zip file with 40 unique reading practice sheets. Each sheet has a selection of 500+ common words on one side and phrases from P.D. Eastman's Picture Dictionary on the other. Print, fold in half and laminate (or use clear-plastic folders). Use along with the Spelling Practice page.
Word Quiz
Players are asked to sight-read words (mostly nouns). There are two game modes: Word → Picture (easier) and Picture → Word (harder).
