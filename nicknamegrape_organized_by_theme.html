Numbers

  • Missing Duck - Help Mother Duck find her missing duckling! (Just press Spacebar to advance the game.)

    • Hungry Hamsters

    Watch these hungry hamsters eat seeds, carrots, and nuts! Try and count how many they can pack in their cheeks.

  • Guess How Many Fruits - A random number of fruits between 100 and 990 will be shown. Use the number keys to make a guess, and press Enter to check if you guessed rightly.
  • Number Treasure Hunt - Dig for buried treasure on numbered islands.

    • Fruits and Vegetables

  • Monkeys and Lion - Monkeys (students) pick fruits from bushes. One of the fruits hides a lion.

    • Ordering Food

  • [2P] Order Food - Share a tablet and take turns ordering food and paying the correct amount.
